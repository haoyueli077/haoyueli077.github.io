Hi! I am Haoyue Li, a fourth-year Ph.D. student in the [Department of Statistics](https://science.psu.edu/stat) at Pennsylvania State University, advised by Dr. [Runze Li](https://science.psu.edu/stat/people/ril4). I earned my M.S. in Biostatistics from the University of Michigan, where I worked with Dr. [Peisong Han](https://sph.umich.edu/faculty-profiles/han-peisong.html) and Dr. [Jeremy Taylor](https://sph.umich.edu/faculty-profiles/taylor-jeremy.html). I received my B.S. in Economics with a minor in Mathematics from Nankai University.

My research focuses on statistical methods for data integration and transfer learning. I am also interested in shrinkage methods, high-dimensional statistics, federated learning, differential privacy, and causal inference.
